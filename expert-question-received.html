After sending your question

If you reached this page after successfully submitting the Ask an Expert form, the form service returned a successful submission response.

Visiting this page directly is not proof that a question was received. A successful submission response does not confirm that Stephen has read your question, that it is within scope or that an expert answer will be provided.

What happens next?

Questions are considered for scope and available capacity. If Stephen can take on your general question, he will reply privately to the email address you supplied. There is no fixed response time, and the pilot may pause intake when capacity is reached.

Any automated acknowledgement is only an administrative receipt. It is not an expert answer or evidence of human review. This page does not confirm that an email has been sent or delivered; if a message is sent, it may appear in your spam or junk folder.

Please do not send documents, health information or identifying details about a child. Your question is not published, and submitting it does not sign you up to marketing.

An urgent issue or deadline cannot wait

Do not wait for an expert reply if you need urgent help or timely individual advice. Sending a question does not pause a deadline or appoint Stephen to manage your case. This is not an emergency or safeguarding service.

Asking a question has not booked a consultation or started paid casework. A free 30-minute consultation and paid services are separate options with their own scope.
