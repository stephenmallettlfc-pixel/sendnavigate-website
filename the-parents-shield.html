A practical introduction to an unfamiliar process

EHCP applications, local-authority decisions and annual reviews involve unfamiliar language and paperwork. The Parent’s Shield explains the EHCP journey in plain English, with practical approaches to preparing information and understanding next steps.

The book covers EHCP requests and reviews, correspondence and evidence preparation, and SEND Tribunal preparation. It is written for parents navigating the SEND system in England; different legal systems apply elsewhere in the UK.

A book cannot review your child’s evidence, establish your individual legal position or guarantee provision. Use current official guidance for procedure and deadlines, and seek appropriate individual advice when needed.

You can start with a book — or with a conversation

If you want help applying general information to the issue you are facing, Stephen offers a free 30-minute consultation plus a written next-step summary. Tell him whether the local authority has refused to assess, refused to issue an EHCP, or whether an existing plan or school support is the concern.

The short summary, normally one to two pages, is based on the discussion only. It records the issue you described, up to three practical next steps or questions, relevant resources and whether further scoped support could help. Delivery timing is agreed on the call.

It is not a document audit, legal merits opinion, drafted letter, appeal-grounds service or ongoing casework. Detailed document reviews are separately agreed paid work. Read the full free-summary scope.
