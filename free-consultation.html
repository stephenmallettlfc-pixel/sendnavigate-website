Your free written next-step summary

The summary is a short follow-up, normally one to two pages, based on what you tell me during the call. It is not a review of documents you have sent or an assessment of the legal merits of your case. We will agree delivery timing during the conversation.

Included in the free summary

  • The main issue as you have described it.
  • Up to three practical next steps or questions to resolve.
  • Relevant general guidance and free-support resources.
  • Whether a separately agreed paid service could be useful, with no obligation to proceed.

Separate paid work, if agreed

  • Pre-reading, detailed EHCP or assessment-report review, and evidence audits.
  • Individual case analysis, drafted letters or appeal grounds.
  • Meeting, mediation or tribunal preparation and representation.
  • Ongoing casework or responsibility for managing deadlines.

The free summary does not include a legal merits opinion, full document audit, personalised correspondence, appeal grounds or ongoing case management. A call or summary does not guarantee an EHCP, an appeal result or a return to school.

A paid document review is a different engagement

The £175 Initial Consultation includes pre-reading of one recent document, a 60-minute call and written next steps, within a total allowance of up to 3.5 hours of professional time. It is not a full case-bundle audit.

A section-by-section EHCP review falls under the separately scoped £550 EHCP Review & Strengthening service, with up to 11 hours of professional time. Other packages have different scope and included time.

We agree the service, what you will receive, the time allowance and the fee before paid work begins. See all consultancy services and fees.

Prepare a brief account, not a case bundle

Think about the main difficulty, what school or the local authority has said, and any approaching decision, meeting or appeal date. You do not need a perfectly organised file or to buy a book first.

Please do not email medical records, a full EHCP or an appeal bundle with your initial enquiry. If we agree further work, we will arrange the appropriate document-transfer route. Read our privacy policy.

A deadline needs attention now

Booking a call does not pause a deadline or appoint SEND Navigate to manage your case. Do not wait for the call or summary to check the current official appeal instructions. Seek timely individual advice where needed.
