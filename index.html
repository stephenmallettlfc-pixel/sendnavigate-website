Questions before you get in touch

Choose a starting point that suits you. Neither a book nor a free call is compulsory before a paid enquiry.

Do I need an EHCP before I can ask for help?

No. You can discuss school SEN Support, an assessment request or a school meeting before a plan exists. We will confirm whether your question fits Stephen’s educational consultancy role.

Do I have to buy a book or book a free call first?

No. The books and free consultation are optional. You can enquire about paid services directly; scope, professional time and fee must be agreed before paid work starts.

Can I ask a general question without a call?

Yes. Ask an Expert is an alternative for a focused general SEND or education question. It is not a full case audit, document review or ongoing casework, and there is no fixed reply-time promise. You do not need to buy a book.
