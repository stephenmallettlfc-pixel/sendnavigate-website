Which problem do you need help with?

Start with the decision or support problem in front of you. Document review, evidence work and drafting below are paid work within an agreed scope, not part of the free summary.

Refusal to assess for an EHCP

We can examine what the LA has decided, what your existing evidence says and which questions need answering. An assessment refusal needs a different approach from a refusal to issue a plan after assessment.

Refusal to issue an EHCP after assessment

We can compare the needs identified during assessment with the recommended provision and the support available without a plan, to help decide whether to challenge the decision.

School distress and difficult attendance conversations

Your child may struggle to enter school, remain in lessons or recover after the school day. I can help you describe concerns, organise relevant information and prepare questions for school or the LA. Consultancy does not diagnose or treat anxiety or other health conditions.

An EHCP or school support plan that is not working

We can distinguish between support that is missing and wording or provision that needs changing. The next step depends on the problem; it will not always be an EHCP appeal.

Preparing for an appeal or tribunal

Where appropriate, we can agree help with evidence, drafting or hearing preparation. Representation is available through the relevant package, not the automatic next step for every refusal.

The free introductory call is a starting conversation, not a full audit of your documents or an appeal-drafting service. Paid work, included professional time and fees are agreed separately.
