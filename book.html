What's in the book

Built from twenty-five years inside the UK education system. Written to help parents organise questions and understand possible next steps. It cannot guarantee an attendance or tribunal outcome.

The Sanctuary Home Protocol

A discussion of lower-demand approaches at home. This is not clinical treatment or a promise of recovery within a set time.

The Won't vs Can't Distinction

Ways to describe barriers to attendance and discuss support without assuming defiance.

The Inarguable Evidence Binder

Ideas for recording relevant observations and questions for school, health professionals and the local authority.

The Section 19 Burden Shift

An introduction to the local authority’s education duties and questions about suitable provision. Eligibility depends on circumstances.

The Parental EHCP Blueprint

How a parent can request an EHC needs assessment directly. Assessment and appeal outcomes are not guaranteed.

EOTAS and the Alternative Routes

An introduction to education otherwise than at school. It is not an automatic entitlement whenever school attendance is difficult.

The First 30 Days Protocol

If you've just bought the book and your child is out today — this page tells you what to do this week.

The Long Game

Longer-term questions about education, support and suitable next steps. There is no single recovery timetable.

The Companion Workbook

Forty pages of the highest-leverage tools from the book — the Three-State Tracker, the Symptom & Impact Log, the fillable Section 19 letter, and Your Situation Map. Free.

About the author

SM

Stephen Mallett is a former Headteacher and SENCo with twenty-five years inside the UK education system. He has led schools through inspection, overseen complex SEND provision, written and defended EHCPs, and made the calls to Local Authorities that parents are usually the wrong side of.

He is also a parent. The framework in this book is the one he reached for when his own family needed it — and the one he could not find on a shelf.

He is the founder of SEND Navigate, which provides specialist EHCP and tribunal advisory support to families and the See It. Support It. Teach It. SEND training programme to schools and MATs.

He is the author of Parent Shield, the first volume in The SEND Navigate Library.
